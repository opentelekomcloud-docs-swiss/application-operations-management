Business Metric Access
======================

You can use ICAgents to report CCE container metrics and ECS metrics to AOM.

Connecting AOM Metrics
----------------------

#. On the menu bar, choose **Monitoring Center**.
#. In the navigation pane, choose **Access Center**.

3. On the **Business** panel, click the **AOM APIs** card and connect metrics as prompted.
